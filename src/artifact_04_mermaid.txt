    %% Flow interactions
    Sender -->|1. Deposit Collateral & Set Rate| InitializeStream
    InitializeStream --> SignerCheck
    SignerCheck --> MathGuard
    MathGuard -->|Initialize PDA State| StreamRegistry
    MathGuard -->|CPI: TransferChecked to Vault| Token2022Program
    Token2022Program -->|Lock Tokens| StreamVault

    Recipient -->|2. Pull Accrued Stream| ClaimFunds
    ClaimFunds --> CliffGuard
    CliffGuard --> ClockSysvar
    CliffGuard --> MathGuard
    MathGuard -->|CPI: TransferChecked PDA Signed| Token2022Program
    Token2022Program -->|Disburse Claimable Balance| RecipientATA

    Sender -->|3. Additional Deposits| TopUpStream
    TopUpStream --> Token2022Program
    Token2022Program --> StreamVault

    Sender -->|4. Revoke / Abort| CancelStream
    CancelStream --> ClockSysvar
    CancelStream --> MathGuard
    MathGuard -->|Refund Unclaimed Remainder| SenderATA
    MathGuard -->|Payout Claimable to Recipient| RecipientATA

    Keeper -->|5. Crank Inactive / Expired Streams| AutoCrank
    AutoCrank --> ClockSysvar
    AutoCrank --> MathGuard
    MathGuard -->|Keeper Tip CPI| KeeperBountyATA
    MathGuard -->|Auto Close PDA & Reclaim Rent| Sender
