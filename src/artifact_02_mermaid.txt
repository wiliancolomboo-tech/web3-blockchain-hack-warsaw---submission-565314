flowchart TD
    %% Actors
    Sponsor[Enterprise Sponsor]
    Contributor[Selected Contributor]
    Arbiter[Institutional Arbiter]
    Contract[Solana Program: Varsovia StreamSafe]
    VaultPDA[Vault Account PDA]
    Listener[Gateway Automation Worker]
    EnterpriseNotifs[Enterprise Notification Matrix: Resend / Slack / Discord]

    %% Step 1: Initialization
    Sponsor -->|1. initialize_escrow: deposit lamports & define milestones| Contract
    Contract -->|Allocates PDA & transfers funds| VaultPDA
    Contract -.->|Emits EscrowInitializedEvent| Listener

    %% Step 2: Submission
    Contributor -->|2. submit_deliverable: milestone_idx, sha256_hash, uri| Contract
    Contract -.->|Emits DeliverableSubmittedEvent| Listener

    %% Step 3: Evaluation
    alt Approval Path
        Sponsor -->|3a. approve_milestone: verifies hash & approves release| Contract
        Contract -->|Transfers milestone tranche lamports| Contributor
        Contract -.->|Emits MilestoneReleasedEvent| Listener
    else Dispute Path
        Sponsor -->|3b. raise_dispute: locks escrow pending arbitration| Contract
        Contract -.->|Emits DisputeRaisedEvent| Listener
        Arbiter -->|3c. resolve_dispute: split or refund funds| Contract
        Contract -->|Distributes lamports to Contributor/Sponsor| VaultPDA
        Contract -.->|Emits DisputeResolvedEvent| Listener
    end

    %% Webhook Processing
    Listener -->|Formats Rich Embeds / JSON Payload| EnterpriseNotifs
    EnterpriseNotifs -->|Dispatches Transactional Emails| Sponsor
    EnterpriseNotifs -->|Broadcasts Alert| Contributor
