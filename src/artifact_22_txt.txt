  [INCIDENT CLASSIFICATION]
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SEV-1: RPC node desync / WebSocket stall
  ACTION: The relay service automatically executes heartbeat reconnections. 
          To force reconnect: `docker restart verigrant-warsaw-relay`.
  
  SEV-2: Evaluator key loss
  ACTION: Multisig quorum threshold (e.g., 2-of-3) prevents deadlock. A single 
          compromised or lost key cannot prevent milestone release or clawback.
  
  SEV-3: Builder deadline breach
  ACTION: Once `Clock::get()?.unix_timestamp >= deadline`, the sponsor initiates 
          `cancel_and_refund()`. Vault lamports return 100% to sponsor wallet.
