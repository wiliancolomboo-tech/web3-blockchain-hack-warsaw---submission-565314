### Incident Response & Runbook

1. **RPC Desynchronization / Drop**:
   - The Relayer handles automatic WebSocket reconnections. If the WebSocket disconnects, run:
     `docker-compose restart solvigil-sentinel`
2. **Disputed Milestone Triage**:
   - Step 1: Admin or Beneficiary inspects event hash payload via the Resend audit email.
   - Step 2: Arbitrator queries on-chain state:
     `solvigil-cli query-escrow -e <ESCROW_PDA>`
   - Step 3: Council casts resolution verdict:
     `anchor run resolve --escrow <PDA> --milestone <IDX> --verdict approve|reject`
3. **Emergency Clawback Procedure**:
   - In case of project abandonment after milestone expiry, run:
     `solvigil-cli clawback --keypair ./admin-keypair.json --escrow-state <ESCROW_PDA>`
